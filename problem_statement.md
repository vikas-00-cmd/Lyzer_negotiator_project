# Problem Statement: Autonomous B2B Supply Chain & SLA Contract Negotiator

## 1. Overview
**Domain:** Enterprise B2B / Procurement / Supply Chain Management
**Objective:** Build a multi-agent B2B negotiation platform utilizing the Lyzr Agent API and Lyzr Automata. Bounded Buyer and Supplier agents will autonomously negotiate price, delivery timelines, and SLA clauses under strict CFO/Legal guardrails, eventually emitting an enforceable contract alongside a tamper-proof audit log.

## 2. Background Context
* **The Problem:** Procurement teams spend thousands of hours manually negotiating spot Purchase Orders (POs), SLA penalties, payment terms, and delivery milestones across highly volatile supplier markets. 
* **The Limitation of Standard AI:** Single LLM prompts fail in this environment. Unbounded agents concede too much too quickly. Real-world negotiations require game-theoretic multi-agent dynamics where every clause, concession, and counter-offer is mathematically bounded and legally auditable.

## 3. Core Architecture
The system must be built around four primary pillars:
1. **Policy Setup:** The Buyer and Vendor define private policy envelopes prior to the negotiation. These envelopes contain strict reservation prices (e.g., maximum budget, walk-away price, delivery windows, and SLA penalty bounds) that must remain hidden from the opposing agent.
2. **Buyer & Vendor Agents:** Isolated agents exchange proposals sequentially. They must utilize private memory and strategies to reach a deal without revealing their absolute bottom lines (BATNA).
3. **Legal Arbiter / Safe AI:** A middleware validation layer. Every counter-offer generated by an agent must be mathematically validated against their private policy envelope. The arbiter blocks any out-of-bounds concessions before they are sent to the opponent.
4. **Consensus & Contract:** Once both agents agree on identical terms, the system compiles the final parameters into an executable contract (structured JSON and a formatted PDF) and logs the full conversational transcript.

## 4. MVP Deliverables
* **Dual-Agent Simulation:** Two distinct agents with private policy envelopes (BATNA, walk-away prices, SLA constraints) communicating via structured payloads.
* **Lyzr Safe AI Hard Guardrails:** Programmatic enforcement ensuring agents cannot violate procurement thresholds (e.g., Buyer cannot offer more than their max budget).
* **Game-Theoretic Negotiation Logic:** Multi-round negotiation featuring convergence strategies, concession curves (agents making smaller concessions over time), and deadlock detection.
* **Legal Contract Generator:** A module that converts agreed terms into a structured JSON payload and outputs a final PDF contract.
* **Governance Log:** Full, tamper-proof negotiation audit trail captured via Lyzr AIMS.

## 5. Stretch Goals (Optional Enhancements)
* **Interactive Arena UI:** A frontend dashboard to visualize live concessions, bid curves, and chat rounds in real-time.
* **Multi-Vendor RFQ:** Escalate the architecture to one Buyer negotiating against three independent Suppliers simultaneously to pick the Pareto-efficient deal.
* **Live Webhook Renegotiation:** Pause and alter ongoing negotiations based on external telemetry (e.g., extending delivery windows due to weather delay webhooks).

## 6. Key Technology Capabilities Required
* **Multi-Agent Environments:** For isolating Buyer/Supplier memory and policy.
* **Safe AI Governance:** For hard numerical and contractual bounds.
* **AIMS:** For creating a verifiable, tamper-proof negotiation history.
